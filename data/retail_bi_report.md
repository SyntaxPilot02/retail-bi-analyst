## Conclusions and recommendations

- The analyzed period includes **87** transactions from **6** unique customers, generating a total revenue of **2,427.75** and an average bill of **27.91**.
- The **Loyal** segment, representing about **100%** of customers, forms a natural pipeline into the Best segment. Structured loyalty programs, upsell paths and cross-selling of higher-margin products can systematically lift their value.
- Track how the distribution across Best, Loyal, Regular and At risk segments evolves over time. A shift towards Best and Loyal indicates effective retention and value management, while growth in At risk requires immediate corrective action.
- Combine RFM trends with product-level analytics (top categories, margins, seasonality) to adjust pricing, assortment and service design in a data-driven way, rather than relying on intuition alone.
